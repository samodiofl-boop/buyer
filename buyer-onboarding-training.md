# Buyer Program Onboarding Training

## 1. Scope and Audience

### Target Roles
| Role | Seniority | Region |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Buyer I | Entry-level (0–2 yrs) | All regions |
| Buyer II | Mid-level (2–5 yrs) | All regions |
| Senior Buyer | Senior (5+ yrs) | All regions |
| Procurement Specialist | Specialist track | All regions |

### Tools Used
- **ERP/Procurement System** – purchase order creation, vendor management, requisitions
- **Sourcing Platform** – RFQ/RFP management, bid comparisons
- **Contract Management System** – contract lifecycle, approval workflows
- **Communication & Collaboration** – email, instant messaging, shared drives
- **Reporting Dashboard** – spend analytics, supplier scorecards, KPI tracking

### Expected Outcomes
Upon completing onboarding, each new buyer will be able to:
- Navigate all required systems independently
- Execute end-to-end purchase order workflows with minimal supervision
- Apply sourcing best practices and negotiate basic vendor terms
- Understand and follow compliance, ethics, and approval policies
- Meet individual productivity targets within 90 days

## 2. Program Structure (Mirrored from Title Onboarding)

### Module Sequence
| # | Module | Format | Duration |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Welcome & Company Overview | Live session + self-paced reading | 4 hrs |
| 2 | Procurement Policy & Compliance | E-learning + quiz | 3 hrs |
| 3 | Systems & Tools Walkthrough | Guided lab + sandbox practice | 6 hrs |
| 4 | Sourcing Fundamentals | Live workshop | 4 hrs |
| 5 | Purchase Order Lifecycle | Job aid + live demo + shadowing | 5 hrs |
| 6 | Vendor Management & Negotiation | Role-play scenarios + debrief | 4 hrs |
| 7 | Contracts & Approval Workflows | Self-paced + observed task | 3 hrs |
| 8 | Reporting & Analytics | Hands-on lab | 2 hrs |
| 9 | Practical Exercises & Simulations | Cohort-based scenarios | 4 hrs |
| 10 | Final Assessment & Certification | Written test + manager sign-off | 2 hrs |

**Total guided hours: ~37 hrs** spread across a 90-day window.

### Session Formats
- **Live Instructor-Led (ILT):** Real-time sessions with Q&A; recorded for async review
- **Self-Paced E-Learning:** Modules with embedded knowledge checks
- **Sandbox Labs:** Hands-on system practice in a non-production environment
- **Shadowing:** Paired with a tenured buyer for observed real-work tasks
- **Role-Play / Scenario Exercises:** Simulated buyer situations with facilitator feedback

### Assessments
- Knowledge checks embedded in every module (minimum 80% to proceed)
- Observed task checklist completed by manager or designated mentor
- Final written assessment (minimum 80% to certify)

### Completion Criteria
- All 10 modules marked complete
- All embedded knowledge checks passed
- Final assessment score ≥ 80%
- Manager sign-off on observed task checklist
- 90-day milestone review completed

## 3. Learning Goals by Phase

### Phase 1 – Foundational Knowledge (Days 1–14)
- Understand the company's procurement strategy and organizational structure
- Learn procurement policies, code of conduct, and compliance requirements
- Gain familiarity with vendor and contract databases
- Navigate core systems at a basic level

### Phase 2 – Systems & Process Training (Days 15–30)
- Execute purchase requisitions and purchase orders end-to-end in the system
- Process quotes and compare vendor bids
- Submit contracts for approval through the correct workflow
- Generate standard reports from the analytics dashboard

### Phase 3 – Live Workflow Practice (Days 31–60)
- Handle assigned real purchase orders under mentor supervision
- Conduct introductory vendor communications and negotiations
- Identify and escalate compliance exceptions
- Collaborate cross-functionally with finance, legal, and operations

### Phase 4 – Performance Readiness (Days 61–90)
- Independently manage a full buyer workload to target throughput
- Resolve routine vendor issues without escalation
- Lead at least one RFQ process end-to-end
- Demonstrate understanding of savings tracking and spend reporting

## 5. 30 / 60 / 90-Day Onboarding Path

### 30-Day Milestones
- [ ] Complete Modules 1–3
- [ ] All system access provisioned and verified
- [ ] Attended at least 2 shadowing sessions with a senior buyer
- [ ] First 1:1 manager check-in completed (use 30-day check-in template)
- [ ] No open compliance training items

### 60-Day Milestones
- [ ] Complete Modules 4–7
- [ ] Independently processed at least 10 purchase orders
- [ ] Completed at least one RFQ with mentor oversight
- [ ] Second manager check-in completed (use 60-day check-in template)
- [ ] Vendor communication assessment passed

### 90-Day Milestones
- [ ] Complete Modules 8–10
- [ ] Final assessment score ≥ 80%
- [ ] Observed task checklist fully signed off by manager
- [ ] Independently carrying full workload at target throughput
- [ ] Third manager check-in / formal readiness review completed

### Shadowing Requirements
| Phase | Required Sessions | With Whom |
|-------|------------------|-----------|
| Days 1–14 | 2 sessions (observe only) | Assigned senior buyer buddy |
| Days 15–30 | 2 sessions (assist, guided) | Assigned senior buyer buddy |
| Days 31–60 | 2 sessions (lead, observed) | Manager or senior buyer |

### Manager Check-In Templates
**30-Day Check-In Topics:**
1. How is the trainee settling into tools and processes?
2. Any access or resource gaps?
3. Confidence level on compliance and policy knowledge?
4. Adjust pacing or support needs?

**60-Day Check-In Topics:**
1. Are live workflow tasks being executed correctly?
2. Quality of POs, sourcing activities, and vendor interactions?
3. Any knowledge gaps identified from Modules 4–7?
4. Readiness for independent workload?

**90-Day Check-In Topics:**
1. Has the trainee met all milestone criteria?
2. Final assessment and observed task checklist review
3. Formal readiness declaration (Ready / Needs Extension)
4. Individual development plan for next 6 months

## 6. Role-Specific Scenarios and Practical Exercises

### Scenario 1 – Emergency Purchase Request
**Situation:** A production line needs a part urgently. The normal 3-quote process cannot be completed in time.  
**Task:** Process an emergency PO using the expedited approval workflow while documenting the justification.  
**Success Criteria:** PO submitted within SLA, correct approval chain used, exception form filed.

### Scenario 2 – Vendor Quote Discrepancy
**Situation:** Two vendors submitted quotes for the same item with a 40% price difference.  
**Task:** Evaluate quotes using the vendor scorecard, request clarification from the higher-priced vendor, and prepare a recommendation memo.  
**Success Criteria:** Scorecard completed, written justification provided, manager approves recommendation.

### Scenario 3 – Contract Renewal Decision
**Situation:** A key supplier contract expires in 30 days. The supplier has requested a 15% price increase.  
**Task:** Review the contract, research market alternatives, and present a negotiation strategy or alternative sourcing recommendation.  
**Success Criteria:** Market research documented, negotiation script prepared, escalation path identified if needed.

### Scenario 4 – Non-Compliant Purchase
**Situation:** You discover a colleague submitted a purchase order that bypassed the approval workflow.  
**Task:** Identify the policy violation, flag it through the correct channel, and correct the PO record.  
**Success Criteria:** Violation correctly identified, escalated to manager, corrective action documented.

### Scenario 5 – Supplier Performance Issue
**Situation:** A supplier has missed three consecutive delivery deadlines.  
**Task:** Pull the supplier scorecard from the analytics dashboard, prepare a performance improvement discussion agenda, and draft a formal notice letter.  
**Success Criteria:** Scorecard data accurate, agenda covers all required points, letter follows contract SLA language.

## 7. Certification and Readiness Gates

### Gate 1 – Compliance Readiness (End of Phase 1, ~Day 14)
| Criteria | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| Module 2 knowledge check | ≥ 80% |
| Code of ethics acknowledgment signed | Required |
| System access verified | Required |

### Gate 2 – Process Readiness (End of Phase 2, ~Day 30)
| Criteria | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| Modules 3–5 knowledge checks | ≥ 80% each |
| Shadowing sessions completed | Minimum 4 |
| PO observed task: create and submit PO | Pass (no critical errors) |

### Gate 3 – Workflow Readiness (End of Phase 3, ~Day 60)
| Criteria | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| Modules 6–7 knowledge checks | ≥ 80% each |
| Vendor communication observed task | Pass |
| Contract workflow observed task | Pass |
| 10+ POs processed independently | Required |

### Gate 4 – Full Certification (End of Phase 4, ~Day 90)
| Criteria | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| Final written assessment | ≥ 80% |
| All observed task checklists signed off | Required |
| Manager readiness declaration | "Ready" |
| 90-day check-in completed | Required |

**Quality Thresholds for Observed Tasks:**
- **Critical Error:** Any compliance violation, unauthorized approval bypass, or data integrity issue → automatic fail; remediation required before re-assessment
- **Major Error:** Incorrect vendor selection documentation, missing required fields → up to 2 allowed; coaching required
- **Minor Error:** Formatting, non-material process deviations → noted but do not block certification

## 8. Success Metrics and Reporting

### Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Description | Target | Reporting Cadence |
|--------|-------------|--------|-------------------|
| Time to Productivity | Days from start date to independent full workload | ≤ 90 days | Per cohort |
| Certification Pass Rate | % of trainees passing final assessment on first attempt | ≥ 85% | Per cohort |
| PO Error Rate | % of trainee POs with critical or major errors in first 30 days of solo work | < 5% | Monthly |
| Compliance Incident Rate | Compliance exceptions attributable to new buyers | 0 critical | Monthly |
| Trainee Confidence Score | Self-reported readiness survey (1–5 scale) at 30/60/90 days | ≥ 4.0 at 90 days | Per check-in |
| Manager Satisfaction Score | Manager-rated readiness at 90-day review (1–5 scale) | ≥ 4.0 | Per cohort |
| Retention Rate | % of buyers still in role at 6 months post-hire | ≥ 90% | Semi-annual |
| Training Completion Rate | % completing all modules on time | ≥ 95% | Per cohort |

### Reporting Process
1. **Training system** auto-tracks module completion, quiz scores, and assessment results
2. **Manager check-in forms** captured in shared tracker (link in onboarding hub)
3. **Monthly report** compiled by Procurement Operations Lead and shared with Procurement Director
4. **Cohort review** held after every new hire class to review KPIs and flag improvement areas

## 9. Pilot Program

### Pilot Cohort
- **Size:** 3–5 new buyer hires
- **Timing:** First available new hire class following program sign-off
- **Facilitator:** Senior Buyer + Training Facilitator (co-delivery)

### Feedback Mechanisms
| When | Method | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| After each module | Short pulse survey (3–5 questions) | Trainee |
| End of Phase 2 (Day 30) | Structured feedback interview | Training Facilitator |
| End of pilot (Day 90) | Full program debrief survey + facilitator retrospective | Training Lead + Manager |

### Pilot Review Criteria
- Were all KPI targets (Section 8) met or on track?
- Were any modules flagged as unclear, too long, or missing content?
- Did any observed task or scenario expose a content gap?
- Was the 30/60/90-day pacing realistic?

### Revision Process
1. Collect all feedback within 2 weeks of pilot completion
2. Training Lead categorizes issues: critical fix / improvement / future enhancement
3. Critical fixes resolved before next cohort; all others logged in backlog
4. Revised program version published with changelog
